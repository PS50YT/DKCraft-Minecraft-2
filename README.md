# DKCraft: Minecraft 2

![thumbail](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/8f85044e5f7581154964c5dd7e463067b3b6c51a_0.webp)

**DKCraft: Minecraft 2** is a Minecraft-style modpack is full of adventures, explorations, and challenges.

The goal isn't to completely change Minecraft into something unrecognizable. Instead, DKCraft expands the Minecraft experience while keeping the original Minecraft feeling at its core.

## 🌎 Explore a Bigger World

Discover new biomes, caves, structures, creatures, and dimensions as you travel through an expanded Minecraft world.

DKCraft includes mods such as:

* 🌿 **Biomes O' Plenty** — Explore a huge variety of new biomes.
* 🌑 **BetterNether** — A greatly expanded Nether experience.
* 🌌 **BetterEnd** — Discover a more detailed and diverse End dimension.
* 🪐 **Ad Astra** — Build rockets and explore beyond the Overworld.
* 🐻 **Alex's Mobs** — Encounter a wide variety of new creatures.
* 🕳️ **Alex's Caves** — Discover mysterious and dangerous underground cave systems.
* 🍳 **Farmer's Delight** — Expand farming, cooking, and food progression.
* 🌊 **Aquamirae** — Experience a darker aquatic adventure with new content.
* 🪽 **Aether** - A completely new dimension of dreams.
* ✨ **& More**

## ⚔️ Adventure & Survival

DKCraft brings together exploration, survival, combat, dimensions, caves, mobs, and progression to create an adventure-focused Minecraft experience.

You'll encounter new environments and creatures while exploring the Overworld, Nether, End, underground caves, Aether and even outer space.

## 🍃 Still Minecraft

Despite the large amount of additional content, DKCraft is designed around the **Minecraft style**.

The goal is to make Minecraft feel **bigger and more adventurous, making the Minecraft Part 2**, rather than turning it into a completely different game.

## 🚀 Optimized for Mid-Range Devices

The modpack is designed to be playable on **mid-range PCs**, while still providing a substantial amount of additional content by using many **optimisation mods**.

Performance can vary depending on your hardware, render distance, shaders, and other settings.
